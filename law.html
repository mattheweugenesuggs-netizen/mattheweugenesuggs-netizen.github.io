The rules behind my daily work

Bank Secrecy Act / AML
Requires financial institutions to help detect and report money laundering and other financial crime, including through Suspicious Activity Reports.
Regulation Z
Carries out the Truth in Lending Act, which requires clear disclosure of loan costs and terms. I worked with it daily as a loan officer.
ECOA
The Equal Credit Opportunity Act bars discrimination in any part of a credit decision.
NCUA
The National Credit Union Administration regulates federal credit unions and insures member deposits at federally insured credit unions.
