Process

How I investigate fraud

Every case follows the same path, from the first alert to the final report. This page describes that process in general terms. Case details stay confidential.

From alert to report

Step 1

Flag

A case starts when something looks wrong: a Verafin alert, an anomaly I catch in daily account monitoring, or a referral from frontline staff.

Step 2

Review the activity

I compare recent activity against the member's normal patterns. I look at IP addresses, device history, address and contact changes, and how fast transactions are moving.

Step 3

Identify the scheme

The pattern usually points to a type: check fraud, ACH fraud, wire fraud or account takeover. Knowing the type tells me what evidence to pull next.

Step 4

Document the evidence

I build a complete case narrative: what happened, when, and the evidence behind each finding, written so someone new to the case can follow it.

Step 5

Escalate and report

I escalate to the fraud management team and senior leadership, and prepare a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) when the activity meets BSA/AML reporting requirements.

Step 6

Track the trend

Each case feeds my Excel reporting on fraud trends for senior leadership. What I learn goes back into staff training, so the next attempt gets caught sooner.

Signals I watch

Account and device

  • IP irregularities
  • Device history changes
  • Unusual address activity

Transactions

  • Transaction velocity
  • Activity outside the member's normal pattern

Tools and frameworks

Verafin
Fraud detection alerts and transaction monitoring
Advanced Excel
Case tracking, fraud trend reporting and compliance metrics
BSA/AML and NCUA
The reporting and internal control rules every case is worked under
